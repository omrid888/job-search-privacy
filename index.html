job-search

job-search is a private, personal tool used by one person (Omri Dahan) on his own computer. It turns his job-alert emails into a daily summary. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

job-search reads job-alert emails in its owner's own Gmail, sends a summary email to that same owner, and updates a Google Sheet he owns.

  • It only reads emails from job sites (such as LinkedIn, Glassdoor and AllJobs).
  • Job descriptions from those emails are sent to Anthropic's Claude to rate how well each job fits. No other email content is sent.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with other third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

job-search's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
